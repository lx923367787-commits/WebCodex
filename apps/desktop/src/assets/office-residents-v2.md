Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool, 2026-10-23. Original retained in the Codex generated-images directory; this project uses `office-residents-v2.png` (1086 × 1448, RGBA, transparent).

## Final generation prompt

Create a production-ready transparent PNG character sprite atlas for an animated desktop AI office. Style: exceptionally pretty premium 3D / 2.5D chibi fashion dolls, fictional young ADULT office friends inspired by polished Chinese beauty-influencer aesthetics (网红感精致Q版): large luminous almond eyes, delicate lashes and brows, refined small nose, soft rosy makeup, glossy natural lips, finely rendered silky hair, cute 2.5-head proportions, sophisticated pastel outfits. Beautiful, charming, tasteful, non-sexual. MUCH richer facial detail and finish than simple SVG mascots, clean readable silhouette at small size. No animal suits, no crude simple shapes, no text, no labels, no grid lines, no watermark, no furniture or props.

ATLAS GEOMETRY IS CRITICAL: exact 3 equal columns x 4 equal rows, twelve cells total, portrait canvas 3:4 aspect ratio. Each cell is square. Every figure is full body including both shoes, centered in its own cell with generous transparent padding, same head size, same camera distance, same foot baseline. Nothing overlaps cell borders. Genuinely transparent alpha background; no checkerboard, no solid color background, no ground plane. Tiny soft contact shadow only.

Each ROW is ONE CONSISTENT CHARACTER repeated in three different animation poses. First column: standing still, cheerful and composed, facing camera with subtle three-quarter turn. Second column: small walking step with LEFT foot forward and arms gently alternating. Third column: opposite walking step with RIGHT foot forward. Keep the face, outfit, hairstyle and scale identical across each row. Do not mirror faces. All poses face mostly forward so facial beauty remains clear.
